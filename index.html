A Private Sanctuary in Pensacola

Ostel Place is a premier private retreat set on over 17 acres of protected land. Designed for families, friends, and meaningful gatherings, our property offers rare privacy and luxury amenities while remaining close to everything Pensacola has to offer.

Outdoor Oasis

Resort-Style Pool & Patio

Our highlight feature is the luxury beach-entry pool with walk-in access—perfect for all ages. Relax on the 1,200 sq ft solar-lit patio, complete with string lights and comfortable seating for evening relaxation.

Rest & Restoration

Two Private Master Suites

Experience ultimate comfort with two king master suites situated on opposite sides of the home. Each features premium bedding and modern ensuite access, providing a spa-like retreat for every guest.

Entertainment

Private Cinema & Media Room

Host a movie night in our dedicated theater room. Featuring a large projection screen, high-end sound, and a wet bar, it’s the perfect hub for rainy days or evening entertainment.

Host Your Meaningful Gathering

Perfect for family vacations, military family visits, micro-weddings, birthday dinners, girls’ weekends, retreats, Blue Angels weekends, and small approved gatherings.
