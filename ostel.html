About This Space

Ostel Place is your private Pensacola retreat—more than a home, it’s a private entertainer’s getaway on over 17 acres of protected land. Tucked at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac and surrounded by conservation wetlands, this property offers rare privacy, peaceful views, and a setting that feels completely removed, yet still close to everything.

The Space

Step inside a home designed for entertaining, relaxing, and hosting with style. The floor plan flows naturally from the kitchen to the living room and outdoor pool area, making it perfect for families, groups, and special gatherings.

  • Two master suites with king beds and ensuite access
  • Chef’s kitchen with quartz island, farmhouse sink, and stainless appliances
  • Cinema room with projection screen, wet bar, and four futons for extra space
  • Indoor/outdoor living with pool views, seating for 16, and a 98-inch smart TV
  • Event-ready property for approved gatherings up to 60 guests (12 overnight)

The Bathrooms

The primary ensuite is a spa-like retreat with a clawfoot tub, rainfall shower, heated toilet seat, and heated towel bar. The second full bathroom offers a tub/shower combo, generous counter space, and a towel warmer. A convenient half-bath with direct pool access includes hooks, racks, and a towel warmer—an underrated family favorite.

The Kitchen & Dining

The chef’s kitchen is equipped with premium stainless steel appliances, an electric range, and a massive island with seating for four. Open sightlines to the pool and living areas keep everyone connected whether you’re cooking, lounging, or dining outdoors.

The Living Area

The main living space is built for gathering and relaxing, with a 98-inch smart TV and an electric fireplace that creates a cozy atmosphere. It’s a welcoming hub where everyone can unwind together.

The Cinema Room

Your personal theater includes a large projection screen, wet bar with sink, and a striking world map mural. It’s perfect for movie nights or rainy afternoons, and the four futons offer extra sleeping space as needed.

The Pool & Outdoor Space

The highlight is the luxury pool with beach-style, walk-in entry—safe for toddlers and easy for guests of all ages. Multi-color LED lighting sets a beautiful evening mood, while the 1,200 sq ft solar-lit patio provides plenty of outdoor seating, shade, and room to entertain.

The fully private wooded yard feels hidden from neighbors, and poolside heaters are available upon request for cooler nights. Please note the yard is not fully fenced, and the privacy-fenced area plus detached garage are not accessible to guests.

Guest Access

You’ll have full, private access to the home during your stay, including driveway parking for 4+ vehicles and keyless entry. Your custom code will be provided before check-in.

Additional parking is available for events up to 60 guests (12 overnight) for a fee. Event tables and chairs are also available for rent—message the host for details.

Other Things to Note

  • In-unit washer/dryer in a dedicated hallway
  • Parking for 4+ vehicles in a private driveway
  • Ceiling fans in every room
  • Statement chandeliers blending glam and country charm
  • Central A/C and heat
  • Events available with host approval and additional fees

Ostel Place isn’t just a rental—it’s a private sanctuary where connection feels effortless and everything flows.
